About the book

Four stories. Four worlds.

In these four powerful short stories, a soldier struggles to return home from the devastation of World War I, carrying the scars of war and memories of a life left behind.

A moving tale of systemic caste discrimination reveals the resilience of those forced to fight against invisible barriers every day, and yet life turns its back on them every single time.

An Indian journalist's experience in Palestine uncovers the complexities of reporting from a land shaped by history, war, and human perseverance.

Finally, a gripping dystopian thriller, set in the near future, follows a man on the run in the woods, far from the chaos of the city, and yet haunted by the memories of dissent—a cautionary tale of freedom of expression, control, surveillance, and fear.

Spanning different eras, settings, and perspectives, this collection examines what it means to endure hardship, seek truth, and hold on to humanity in a changing world.

Thought-provoking, emotional, and immersive, these stories invite readers to reflect on the forces that shape lives and the courage required to confront them.

From the book
