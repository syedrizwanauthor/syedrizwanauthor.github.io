About the author

Syed Rizwan

Novelist, short story writer, and poet from Maharashtra, India.

Syed Rizwan writes fiction in English and poetry in Urdu and Hindi. His work is interested in memory, mortality, alienation, social prejudice, history, and the private worlds people build in order to survive the public one.

He holds a Master’s degree in English Literature and began writing short fiction in English in 2021. His books include Heaven Under the Rubble and Memories of the Setting Sun.
