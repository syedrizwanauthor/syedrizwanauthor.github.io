About the author

Syed Rizwan

Novelist, short story writer, and poet from Maharashtra, India.

Syed Rizwan writes fiction in English and poetry in Urdu and Hindi. His work is interested in memory, magical realism, alienation, social prejudice, history, and ordinary people and their extraordinary journeys.

He holds a Master’s degree in English Literature and began writing short fiction in English in 2021. His books include Heaven Under the Rubble, Memories of the Setting Sun, and Zeena Zeena Utar Rahi Hai Raat, a collection of poetry in Urdu and Hindi.
