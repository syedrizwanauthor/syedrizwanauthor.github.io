Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information I collect

When you place an order for a signed book through this website, you may be asked to provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, PIN code, and the name you would like used for your autograph.

How I use your information

The information you provide is used only for purposes such as processing your order, arranging delivery, contacting you about your order, and providing the signed copy you requested.

Payment information

Payments for signed books are processed through Razorpay. Payment details such as card, bank, or UPI information are handled by the payment provider and are not stored by this website.

Newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to my newsletter, you will be directed to Substack, where your subscription is managed according to Substack's privacy practices.

Sharing your information

I do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to process your order or deliver your book.

Data retention

Order information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil orders, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

If you have questions about the personal information you have provided, or would like to request its correction or deletion where applicable, you can contact me using the email address below.
