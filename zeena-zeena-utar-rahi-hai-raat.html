Zeena Zeena Utar Rahi Hai Raat

Urdu aur Hindi shayari, Devanagari mein.

Yeh kavya-sangrah Urdu aur Hindi shayari ki us rooh se prerit hai, jahan ehsaas alfaaz ka roop lete hain aur khamoshiyon ki apni ek kahani hoti hai.

Kitaab ke baare mein

Kuch ehsaas, kuch khamoshiyan.

In ghazalon aur nazmon mein mohabbat ki narmi hai, judaai ki kasak hai, umeed ki roshni hai aur saath-saath dard aur tanhaai ki goonj.

Kahin beete lamhon ki parchhaiyan hain, to kahin khwabon aur haqeeqat ke darmiyan chalti ek khamosh guftagu.

Har rachna insani jazbaat ki kisi na kisi parat ko chhooti hai aur paathak ko apne hi dil ke bheetar safar karne par majboor kar deti hai.

Devanagari lipi mein prastut yeh sangrah un paathakon ke liye hai jo shayari ko keval padhte nahin, balki use mehsoos karte hain.

Yeh ek aisi adabi peshkash hai jo har baar padhi jaane par naye maayne aur naye ehsaas chhod jaati hai.

Kitaab se

“Kuch siyaahi se bhare panne nahin hain inqilaab
Ek halchal hai jise sadkon pe laana chahiye”

Kitaab kahan milegi

Apni prati chunein.
